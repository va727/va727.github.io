Hydraulic System

  • Pressure due to a force applied on an enclosed liquid is transmitted equally to all other parts of the liquid

    P by small piston on fluid=P by fluid on large piston

    Ps = Pl

  • Hydraulic fluid must be incompressible and enclosed

    Q:Why must the fluid be incompressible?

    Ans:So that any increase in pressure at a point within the fluid can be transmitted equally to all points within the fluid

Fun Fact!

Did you know why are we able to drink using a straw?

  1. When we suck air out of the straw, it reduces the gas pressure in the straw above the drink so that it is less than the Patm that acts on the surface of the drink outside the straw.

  2. The drink is forced to flow from region of higher pressure towards the region of lower pressure, and thus flows into and up the straw into our mouth

Kinetic Model Particle

State the Kinetic Theory of Matter

All the matter is made up of tiny particles,called atoms or molecules,which are in continuous random motion

Force of attraction between particles,arrangment and movement of particles in....

Solid Liquid Gas
  • Very strong force of attraction between particles.
  • Particles are very closely packed in a regular arrangement.
  • Particles only vibrate about their fixed positions.
  • Strong force of attraction between particles.
  • Particles are closely packed in a random arrangement.
  • Particles can move and slide over one another.
  • Very weak force of attraction between particles.
  • Particles are far apart from one another.
  • Particles move freely and randomly in all directions.

  • Q:Why do solids have fixed shape?

    Particles in solids have very strong FOABP that hold them in regular and closely packed arrangement so that they can only vibrate in fixed position

  • Q:Why are solids and liquids incompressible?

    Solid and liquid particles are closely packed, and thus cannot be pushed to move even closer to one another
